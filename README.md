## Benjamin E. Bagozzi

Professor of Political Science & International Relations, University of Delaware.

My research develops statistical and computational methods for political data that are flawed in systematic ways, such as inflated, underdispersed, misclassified, or underreported outcomes. I turn those methods into open-source software and help produce public datasets.

### R packages

| Package | What it does | Links |
|---|---|---|
| [underdisp](https://github.com/bagozzib/underdisp) | Diagnostics and models for underdispersed count data | [CRAN](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=underdisp) · [docs](https://bagozzib.github.io/underdisp/) |
| [iop](https://github.com/bagozzib/iop) | Inflated ordered probit and logit models | [CRAN](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=iop) · [docs](https://bagozzib.github.io/iop/) |
| [polychoice](https://github.com/bagozzib/polychoice) | Multinomial choice models, inflated multinomial logit, and tests of IIA | In development |
